# Reusable Prospect-Research Prompt

You are compiling a high-quality target list for outbound research.

## Inputs

- Target count: [TARGET_COUNT]
- Geographic scope: [REGION]
- Sender profile or résumé: [SENDER_PROFILE]
- Target customer or employer type: [TARGET_TYPE]
- Relevant capabilities or offer: [CAPABILITIES]
- Preferred company size or stage: [SIZE_OR_STAGE]
- Exclusions: [EXCLUSION_LIST]

## Goal

Identify organizations where the sender's verified background or offer has a credible connection to a current business need. Prioritize evidence and fit over filling the requested count.

Do not infer skills or experience that are not present in the supplied sender profile.

## Strong-fit signals

Look for public evidence such as:

- relevant hiring activity;
- product launches or operational expansion;
- acquisitions or integration work;
- infrastructure, workflow, compliance, or scaling challenges;
- public technology choices;
- team growth in the relevant function;
- job descriptions matching the sender's verified capabilities; and
- credible buyer or recruiter roles connected to the campaign goal.

## Exclusions

Exclude:

- organizations outside the requested geography or target definition;
- duplicate organizations;
- entries on the supplied exclusion list;
- companies with no verifiable reason for inclusion;
- organizations whose websites or identities cannot be confirmed; and
- speculative matches added only to reach the requested count.

## Verification rules

- Verify the official company website.
- Verify the LinkedIn company page when available.
- Use current public evidence.
- Separate observed facts from inference.
- Never fabricate hiring, technology, funding, or operational claims.
- Keep personal data limited to professionally relevant public information.

## Output

Return only these columns:

- Company Name
- Official Website
- LinkedIn Company Page
- Company Type
- Relevant Public Signal
- Likely Need
- Best Contact Function
- Geographic Scope
- Evidence URL
- Fit Rationale

Keep the fit rationale to one or two evidence-based sentences.
